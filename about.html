Hello, I’m Raiqua Maryam.
Hellooo

I started the Envoi Society because I missed getting real mail. Not bills or automated bank letters, but a letter from someone who sat down and thought of me.

Hellooo

So I decided to be that person for others. Each month, I write a letter by hand, choose a few little goodies I think you’ll enjoy, and send it out into the world. It’s slow, it’s personal, and it’s one of my favorite parts of the month.

Hellooo

What a letter from me looks like...

Hellooo

Each letter is written individually, not copied from a template. I write about what I’ve been reading, making, eating, thinking, and noticing. Sometimes there’s a story, sometimes a recipe, sometimes just a ramble about the weather. Every envelope includes a few goodies, and I’ll never tell you what’s coming.

Hellooo

What I believe...

Hellooo

Slow is a gift. Waiting for something makes it feel more special.

Handwriting is personal. Every pen stroke carries a little of the person who wrote it.

Small things matter. A sticker, a dried flower, or a doodle in the margin can brighten a day.

Mail should be joyful. Your mailbox deserves better than junk.

Hellooo

Who it’s for...

Hellooo

Anyone who loves paper, pens, stationery, journals, stamps, or simply the feeling of finding something in the mailbox that’s just for you. It also makes a lovely gift for someone who could use a bit of kindness each month.

Hellooo
